# Figure legends — simulated study

**All observations, donor IDs, protein IDs and numerical results below are simulated solely to demonstrate publication figure design. These are not experimental findings.** “Compound X” is a placeholder. Source CSVs and the simulation model are included; replace them and revise the study design before using this template for research.

## Figure 1 | Paired-donor kinase assay characterization

**a,** Concentration-response profile for Compound X. Activities are expressed as a percentage of the vehicle activity from the same donor. Points and shading indicate the mean and pointwise 95% Student t confidence interval (CI), respectively, across 12 simulated donors. Lines connect measured concentrations; no dose-response model was fitted, and no IC50 was estimated. **b,** Paired kinase activity in vehicle and Compound X conditions. Each point represents one donor; lines connect paired observations. Black summaries show the mean and 95% t CI for each condition. The displayed P value is calculated by a two-sided paired t-test (12 donor pairs, 11 degrees of freedom); this is the single prespecified comparison in this demonstration. **c,** Reaction progress over time. Points and shading show the mean and pointwise 95% t CI across the same 12 donors per condition. Each donor contributes repeated measurements; time points are not independent replicates. Simulated near-zero readings include measurement noise. No tests at individual time points were performed. **d,** Candidate-versus-reference assay agreement. Each point is the donor mean of two technical measurements per assay; n = 12 independent donor units, not 24. Dashed line, identity. Pearson correlation is descriptive and does not establish agreement. **e,** Bland–Altman display of the donor-averaged measurements in d. Solid line, mean difference (candidate minus reference); dashed lines, estimated 95% limits of agreement, calculated as bias ± 1.96 sample SD. These limits are not confidence intervals and do not define an acceptable measurement error; no prespecified acceptance margin is available. **f,** Donor-specific activity reduction, calculated as 100 × (1 − treated/vehicle). Black line and grey band show the mean and its 95% t CI across donors; all donor values are retained in ID order.

Titration, paired-condition and reaction-progress tables are separately simulated design fixtures; they are not a jointly fitted kinetic model. No observations were excluded. Grey denotes vehicle and blue denotes Compound X. Exact calculated statistics are provided in `data/assay_statistics.json`. The t intervals and tests assume approximately normal donor-level values or paired differences; the simulation is not evidence that these assumptions hold for a future empirical dataset.

## Figure 2 | Paired proteomic response to Compound X

**a,** Volcano plot of 420 simulated protein features measured in 12 paired donors. For each feature, log2 fold change is the mean donor-specific difference between Compound X and vehicle log2 intensities. Two-sided one-sample t-tests were applied to these paired differences (n = 12, df = 11), followed by Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment across all 420 features. Dashed lines indicate adjusted P = 0.05 and mean log2 fold changes of −0.5 and +0.5. Blue and orange indicate features meeting both the adjusted-P and magnitude criteria; grey indicates all other features, including features meeting only one criterion. Counts are computed from the CSVs. **b,** Mean paired log2 fold changes and pointwise 95% t CIs for the eight features with the smallest adjusted P values. Features were selected using the same dataset; intervals are not simultaneous or adjusted for selection and are displayed descriptively. Dashed line, no change. **c,** Empirical cumulative distributions of donor-level median log2 protein intensity, using all 420 features within each donor and condition. Each condition has 12 donor summaries; proteins are not treated as independent biological replicates. This panel is descriptive, with no distributional test. **d,** Donor-specific log2 fold changes for the 16 features with the smallest adjusted P values, sorted by adjusted P then identifier; donor columns are in ID order. No row scaling, clustering, imputation or filtering was applied. The diverging color scale is centred at zero and clipped at ±1.5; triangular colorbar extensions indicate values outside this range. All numerical values remain available in the source table.

P0001–P0420 are anonymous simulated feature identifiers, not assignments to real proteins. No missing values or exclusions were introduced. Exact results for every feature, including unadjusted P, adjusted P, n, effect size and CI, are provided in `data/protein_statistics.csv`. The heatmap and selected-effect panel are exploratory summaries of the same data and do not constitute independent validation.
